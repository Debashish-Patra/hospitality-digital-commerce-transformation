# Digital Commerce Transformation: From Platform Modernization to Growth

## Overview

This case study reflects my experience leading the evolution of a large-scale hospitality transformation across business processes, reservation technology, customer experience, and digital commerce.

My responsibilities expanded from Business Process stream ownership and operating-model transformation to reservation-system migration leadership, ultimately encompassing customer experience strategy across room and non-room products and loyalty.

The case study presents an independently developed set of frameworks and illustrative examples to demonstrate how enterprise transformation can connect platform capabilities with customer value and commercial growth.

## Core Leadership Challenge

How can a complex, process-led technology transformation evolve into a customer-centric digital commerce strategy that improves product discovery, purchasing experiences, and monetization?

## Approach

1. Establish the target operating model and understand end-to-end business processes.
2. Translate business needs into platform capabilities and migration priorities.
3. Map customer journeys and identify product discovery and purchasing opportunities.
4. Develop product propositions and commercialization strategies.
5. Define success metrics, governance, and continuous optimization.

## Portfolio Scope

This repository contains original frameworks, hypothetical scenarios, and reusable product strategy tools. It does not reproduce client materials, proprietary data, or confidential system designs.
