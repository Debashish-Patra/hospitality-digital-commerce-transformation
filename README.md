# Digital Commerce Transformation
### From operating-model redesign to platform modernization, customer experience and growth

**Portfolio focus:** Product Strategy · Digital Commerce · Product Marketing · Platform Transformation · Customer Experience · Growth Strategy

## Overview

This portfolio case study demonstrates a repeatable approach to transforming a complex hospitality business from process-led operations toward a more integrated, customer-centric digital commerce experience.

The professional experience behind this case study progressed through three connected areas:
1. **Business process and operating-model transformation** — understanding end-to-end processes, roles, service requirements and operational dependencies.
2. **Reservation-platform migration** — connecting business needs with future-state platform capabilities and cross-functional delivery.
3. **Customer experience and digital commerce strategy** — shaping the experience across room and ancillary products, digital merchandising, upselling and loyalty.

To protect client and employer confidentiality, this repository does **not** reproduce client documents, internal slides, proprietary architecture, confidential metrics or named-system designs. The frameworks and examples below are independently written and illustrative. Where a hypothetical business scenario is used, it is explicitly identified as such.

## The transformation thesis

A platform modernization creates more value when it is designed around the customer and the commercial proposition—not treated as a technology migration alone.

## My leadership lens

The central leadership challenge is to connect three perspectives that are often managed separately:

- **Operations:** Can the business reliably deliver the promised experience?
- **Technology:** Can the platform support the required products, rules, data and transactions?
- **Commerce:** Can customers discover, understand, purchase and receive the product with confidence?

The work is not complete when a feature is released. It is complete when the customer proposition, operating process, platform capability and measurement model work together.
