# Content and confidentiality checklist

- No internal client slides, screenshots, diagrams, templates or extracts are included.
- No client-confidential metrics, internal system names, roadmaps or architecture are disclosed.
- All hypothetical scenarios and numbers are clearly labeled.
- Personal contributions are described accurately and are distinguishable from wider program outcomes.
- No client logo, branding or endorsement is implied without permission.
- Any public claims are supportable and cleared for disclosure where required.
- Every image or infographic is original work. Do not crop, redraw, trace or screenshot client slides, dashboards or system diagrams.
- Infographics show generic frameworks only: no client names, internal system names, dollar figures, owners or dates from a real program.
